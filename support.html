Support & Feedback

Yunxu Chat · Batch export, download and local archiving of AI chat history

If you run into batch export failures, ZIP download problems, incomplete conversation detection, Markdown / JSON / PDF issues, or compatibility changes on an AI platform, you can contact the developer through the channels below.

Microsoft Edge Add-onsEmail: 9072686@qq.com

Support channels are optional. You do not need WeChat or a public-account follow to use Yunxu Chat. WeChat is an optional Chinese-language community/support channel and is not part of extension sign-in, permissions, batch export, or local archiving.

What to include in a batch-export report

  • Browser (Microsoft Edge / Chrome) and version.
  • The AI website where the issue occurred, and whether it was a single export or batch export.
  • The approximate batch range, such as the number of conversations selected. You do not need to send conversation text.
  • What happened: history could not be enumerated, processing stopped, ZIP was not generated, some conversations failed, or another symptom.
  • You may export a local diagnostic JSON from About → Export diagnostics. Diagnostics are designed not to include conversation text, but please review the file before sending it.
Privacy & securityPlease do not send passwords, cookies, tokens, verification codes, private keys, or a complete sensitive chat history through email, WeChat, or other support channels. If you need to send a screenshot or diagnostic file, remove unnecessary personal information first.

批量导出问题反馈时，最好提供这些信息

  • 浏览器（Microsoft Edge / Chrome）及版本。
  • 发生问题的 AI 网站，以及是“单个导出”还是“批量导出”。
  • 批量任务的大致范围，例如选择了多少个会话；无需发送聊天正文。
  • 实际表现：无法枚举历史、处理停住、ZIP 未生成、部分会话失败，或其他现象。
  • 可在“关于 → 导出诊断信息”生成本地诊断 JSON。诊断日志按设计不记录聊天正文，但发送前仍请自行检查文件内容。

数据边界

扩展不会自动上传诊断文件或聊天正文。只有当你主动通过邮件、微信等渠道发送材料时，开发者才会看到你选择提供的内容。支持沟通不会改变云序对话“聊天正文不上传云序服务器”的产品边界。
